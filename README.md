# Sandy Springs Relocation Guide

A single-page guide comparing towns around Sandy Springs, GA: commute, housing under $350K, safety, hospitals, taxes, and a day in the life for each town.

Open `index.html`, or view it on GitHub Pages.

Research snapshot from November 14, 2026. Commute times are estimates. Not financial, tax or legal advice.

Photos are from Wikimedia Commons. Credits and licenses are listed at the bottom of the page.
